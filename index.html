Our Heritage & Coffee Reverence

Born From An Uncompromising Pursuit Of Perfection

Founded in 2018 by award-winning roast masters and passionate culinary dreamers, Café Bliss began as an intimate neighborhood corner with one noble mission: to elevate everyday dining into an extraordinary sensory ritual.

Today, we partner directly with multigenerational coffee estates in Yirgacheffe, Huila, and Antigua — sourcing high-altitude single-origin beans at fair trade premiums. Every dawn before the city stirs, our bakers knead wild levain sourdough and bake pure butter viennoiserie, filling our sunlit space with unforgettable warmth.
